## Article body

After years of working with distributors in more than 35 countries, the same five questions arrive in almost every first conversation. Here are straight answers.

### 1. What is your minimum order?

Our standard export minimum is USD 1,500 on a free on board basis. That is deliberately accessible, so a distributor can test a market with a mixed first order instead of committing to a full container.

Branded or custom items usually need higher quantities per item because of printing and packaging. We agree those minimums for each program.

### 2. What certifications and documents do you provide?

MEDILAB operates a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015. For measuring glassware we can supply batch documentation, and our NABL-accredited calibration supports orders that need individual certificates with a stated uncertainty.

Tell us the standard and class you need on the purchase order, such as ISO 1042 or ASTM E288, and we will confirm the documents that go with it. That one line prevents most disputes.

### 3. Can you make products under my brand?

Yes. We undertake customized and OEM manufacturing and support bulk tender requirements. A typical program starts with a narrow range, approved artwork and samples, then a pilot order before volume.

Your certificates can carry your format as long as traceability to the manufacturer stays available for auditors.

### 4. What can I buy from one supplier?

Glassware, plasticware, general labware including porcelain, microscopes and optical equipment, physics instruments, laboratory equipment, biology models and slides, and analytical instruments. Mixed orders reduce freight per item and simplify customs entry, so many distributors build a first shipment across several categories.

### 5. How do you pack and ship?

Each piece is protected by cells or sleeves, grouped in partitioned cartons, packed in strong outer cartons and palletized where the load needs it. Cartons carry product code, quantity, lot number and handling marks.

We work to the Incoterm you choose and share shipping documents before dispatch. Tell us your destination and mode, sea or air, and we will adapt the packing. Lead time depends on whether items are in stock or made to order, and we confirm it in the quotation.

### A sixth question worth asking

*How do I verify what you tell me?* Ask for the certificate numbers and check them. Request samples. Place a small trial order and compare it with the second one. Consistency between the first and second shipment is the real quality signal, and we would rather earn your trust that way than by promises.

### For US and China readers specifically

- **USA:** confirm the country-of-origin marking, the HS code and the current duty position with your customs broker before fixing a delivered price.
- **China:** confirm filing and labeling requirements with your customs agent, and agree a primary communication channel early.
