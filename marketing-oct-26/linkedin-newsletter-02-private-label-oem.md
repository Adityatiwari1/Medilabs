## Article body

If you resell someone else's brand, you compete with everyone else who resells it.

The customer sees an identical product and picks the lowest quote. Loyalty belongs to the brand owner, and your margin is whatever the market leaves you.

Distributors who sell their own brand change that. The customer's relationship is with you, and you control the price structure, the packaging, the documents and the roadmap.

### Why this works in laboratory glassware

Laboratory glassware is a standardized product. A flask made to a recognized ISO or ASTM standard performs the same whichever name is printed on it. What differs is the quality system behind it. That is why your choice of manufacturing partner matters more than the logo.

### Private label versus OEM, in one line each

- **Private label:** you choose products from the manufacturer's range and sell them under your brand.
- **OEM:** the manufacturer builds to your specification, which can include custom sizes, markings and packaging.

Many programs start as private label and move toward OEM as the brand grows.

### Five decisions that shape a program

1. **Starting range.** Start narrow. Beakers, Erlenmeyer flasks, graduated cylinders, volumetric flasks and funnels have steady demand and easy specification.
2. **Specification.** Name the glass type, the standard, the tolerance class, the marking and the acceptance criteria in writing. One master document, versioned and dated.
3. **Brand and artwork.** Choose a marking method that survives washing and autoclaving. Approve a proof before any production.
4. **Documents.** Decide which certificates carry your name and keep traceability to the manufacturer available for auditors.
5. **Packaging and labeling.** Protective design first, branding second. Include a lot number so complaints can be traced.

### Two market notes

**USA.** Imported goods must show the country of origin, and the importer of record carries compliance responsibility. Customers expect complete documentation and predictable replenishment. A well-run private label line can compete on service even against famous brands.

**China.** Buyers are used to strong domestic production, so imported ranges win on calibration, consistency and documentation. Register your trademark early, because the first applicant often prevails, and check customs filing and labeling rules with your agent before printing cartons.

### Start small, then scale

Run a pilot order. Choose a small, representative mix of items, with full artwork and packaging, and track lead time, quality, documents and freight. Treat it as a rehearsal for the first commercial order.
