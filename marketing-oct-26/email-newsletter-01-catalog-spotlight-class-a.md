## Sending checklist

- **USA recipients (CAN-SPAM):** include the physical address, a clear unsubscribe link, an accurate sender name and subject, and honor opt-outs promptly.
- **China recipients:** send only to contacts who have shared their address with you for business purposes or opted in, in line with China's personal information protection law. Chinese mail providers filter aggressively, so keep the message light and text-forward.
- **Authentication:** publish SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the sending domain. Large mailbox providers require these for bulk senders and expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **Tracking:** UTM tags are included in the links above.
- **Segmenting:** use the same message for all segments. Change only the greeting and, for China, offer a Simplified Chinese version if available.
