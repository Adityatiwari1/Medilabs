## 11. Sections Covered

1. **Why the China-vs-Europe Framework Is Outdated** - Reframes the sourcing decision from binary to three-way, explains how India's manufacturing sector matured to compete on certification rather than price alone.

2. **Reason 1: Quality Parity with European Manufacturing Standards** - Defines quality parity as a verifiable ISO 4787/borosilicate 3.3 claim, not a marketing statement; links to quality standards guide.

3. **Reason 2: A Significant Cost Advantage Without the Quality Trade-off** - Cost structure comparison rationale, relevance to large-volume institutional and tender-based procurement.

4. **Reason 3: Post-Pandemic Supply Chain Diversification** - The "China plus Europe plus India" framing, single-country concentration risk lesson from 2020-2022 disruptions.

5. **Reason 4: Indian Government Export Support** - Make in India initiative, export incentive schemes, IBEF trade data reference.

6. **Reason 5: An English-Language Business and Documentation Environment** - Practical communication and documentation advantage distinct from quality claims.

7. **Reason 6: A Verifiable Multi-Decade Export Track Record** - Medilab's own 40-country/60-year history as a proof point, framed as evidence to verify per-supplier rather than accept as sector-wide claim.

8. **India vs China vs Europe: A Sourcing Comparison** - 6-factor comparison table (cost, certification availability, business language, government support, diversification value, freight time).

9. **How to Add an India Lab Equipment Supplier to Your Sourcing Mix** - Practical qualification guidance (small category first, full remote evaluation framework), contains internal links to blog-14, blog-27, and blog-28.

10. **FAQ** - 7 questions covering quality equivalence, full replacement vs diversification framing, government programs, verification method, cost competitiveness vs China, lead times, and Medilab's own positioning.
