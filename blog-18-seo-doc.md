8. **Check 7: Stopper, Cap, and Closure Fit Assessment** - Ground glass joint fit protocol; threaded cap thread engagement test; closure performance criteria.
9. **Check 8: Batch Documentation and ISO Certification Review** - Required documentation set; lot-level traceability requirements; ISO standard reference verification.
