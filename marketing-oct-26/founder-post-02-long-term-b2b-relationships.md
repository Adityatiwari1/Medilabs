## Post text

The first order is a test. The tenth order is a relationship.

After more than three decades in laboratory manufacturing, this is what I have learned about long-term B2B partnerships:

1. Do the boring things perfectly.
Correct counts. Matching documents. Carton labels that are right. No customer ever thanked us for a clean packing list, and no customer ever forgave a wrong one.

2. Tell people early when something is wrong.
A delay announced on day one is a conversation. A delay discovered on arrival is a complaint.

3. Make it easy to start small.
A distributor who trusts you with a small order today will trust you with a container next year. Our minimum order is low on purpose.

4. Quality is the real price.
The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest outcome once breakage, rejected lots and lost time are counted.

5. Treat every market as a different customer.
A buyer in the USA and a buyer in China need different things, from documents to communication style. Listening comes before selling.

6. Your customers' customers are your customers too.
When a distributor's laboratory client is happy, we find out. When one is not, we find out faster.
