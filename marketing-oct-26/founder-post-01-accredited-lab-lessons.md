## Post text (copy from the line below)

We used to say our glassware was accurate.

Then we built a calibration laboratory that had to prove it.

Here is what that taught us about quality:

1. Accreditation makes you write down what you actually do. Not what you intend to do. What you do, every time, in a form an outside assessor can check.

2. Honest numbers beat impressive numbers. A measurement without its uncertainty is half a measurement. We learned to state the doubt next to the result.

3. Traceability is a chain, and it is only as strong as its weakest link. A balance, a thermometer, a technique at the meniscus. Each one has to be traceable and each one has to be right.

4. Quality is not a department. It is a habit that survives a busy week, a rushed shipment and an unhappy customer on the phone.

5. The paperwork is part of the product. A buyer on another continent cannot visit our factory. The certificate is how they meet us.
