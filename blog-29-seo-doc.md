## 11. Sections Covered

1. **Introduction (3 paragraphs)** - Frames the glossary's purpose: translating supplier and certificate shorthand into plain language for first-time glassware buyers.

2. **A-Z Glossary Body (18 letter groups, 54 terms)** - Alphabetical entries covering material science terms (borosilicate 3.3, annealing, tempering, thermal shock resistance, coefficient of thermal expansion), precision/tolerance terms (Class A, Class B, tolerance, calibration, meniscus, TC, TD, drainage time), standards terms (ISO 4787, ISO 1042, ISO 385, ISO 9001, ISO 17025, ASTM E288, DIN, USP), item types (beaker, burette, conical flask, graduated cylinder, round bottom flask, filter flask, reagent bottle, volumetric flask), and procurement terms (batch compliance certificate, Certificate of Analysis, Certificate of Conformance, materials certificate, MOQ, lead time, OEM).

3. **Standards Quick Reference Table** - 7-standard lookup table mapping ISO/ASTM standard numbers to governing body, applicable item category, and what each standard defines.

4. **How to Use This Glossary When Reviewing a Quote or Certificate** - Practical application guidance, contains all 3 internal links (quality standards, remote supplier evaluation, lab supplier consolidation).

5. **FAQ** - 7 questions covering tolerance class importance, TC vs TD distinction, Pyrex vs borosilicate naming, ISO 9001 vs product-level certification, inclusion of procurement terms, glossary maintenance cadence, and requesting new term additions.
