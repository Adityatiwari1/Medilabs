## 11. Sections Covered

1. **Why Remote Supplier Evaluation Is Now the Standard, Not the Exception** - Reframes remote evaluation as stronger evidence than a single factory visit, sets up the sequential 7-step structure.

2. **Step 1: Request and Audit Core Documentation** - What certificate package to request, what a genuine certificate contains, why to request product-specific rather than company-level certificates, batch record specificity.

3. **Step 2: Independently Verify Compliance Certificates** - How to check certificates against issuing body registries (IAF CertSearch), verifying the certification body's own accreditation status.

4. **Step 3: Run a Live Video Factory Walkthrough** - Requesting live vs pre-recorded video, specific areas to ask for, watching for consistency between sales claims and production floor reality.

5. **Step 4: Order and Test a Paid Sample Batch** - Why a paid sample beats a free sample, what to test on receipt (gravimetric verification, material consistency, packaging quality), comparing sample against batch certificate.

6. **Step 5: Check References from Existing Institutional Buyers** - How many references to request, what operational questions to ask, why asking about past problems is more informative than satisfaction alone.

7. **Step 6: Review Freight and Logistics Track Record** - Requesting sample export documents, asking about past freight disruptions and resolution, assessing customs documentation accuracy.

8. **Step 7: Confirm Communication Reliability Before the First Bulk Order** - Communication stress test methodology, why this step catches gaps the other six steps miss.

9. **Red Flags vs Green Flags Checklist** - Comparison table across all 6 evaluation areas with concrete red flag / green flag pairs.

10. **Applying This Framework to Your Next Sourcing Decision** - Sequencing guidance (cheap checks first), contains all 3 internal links including the new lab-supplier-consolidation post.

11. **FAQ** - 7 questions covering safety of remote-only evaluation, certificate authenticity checks, video call focus areas, paid sample necessity, reference check volume, handling supplier refusal of verification steps, and Medilab's own remote evaluation support.
