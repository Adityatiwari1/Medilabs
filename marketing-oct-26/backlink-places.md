# Free Backlink Places for the Blogs

Follow status and authority figures come from SEO roundups and were not verified on the live pages. Before relying on any place, view the page source of one live post and look for rel="nofollow" on the outbound link, then check the domain's DR in Ahrefs' free backlink checker.

| # | Place | Follow status (per sources) | Approx. authority | How to use the blogs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Substack | Dofollow | DA 92 | Publish the 5 LinkedIn newsletter editions as "MEDILAB Export Insights", linking to the matching blog. Best fit. |
| 2 | Hashnode | Dofollow | DA 88 | Post a condensed version of each blog with the canonical URL set to your own page. Best for blogs 35, 37, 38. |
| 3 | Dev.to | Dofollow | High | Same canonical approach. Technical audience, so only the process-heavy blogs (37, 38). |
| 4 | Blogger | Dofollow | DA 95 | Short unique summary of each blog with a link to the full article. Free subdomain, so lower value than an editorial link. |
| 5 | Crunchbase | Mixed (sources disagree) | DA 91 | One company profile with the website link. Check the link attribute after creating it. |
| 6 | Qwoted | Dofollow when a publisher includes a link | Publisher's DR | Free tier: answer journalist requests on lab supplies, calibration or exporting and cite blog 33 or 38. |
| 7 | Source of Sources | Same as Qwoted | Publisher's DR | Free, HARO-style format. Answer with a short quote plus the matching blog link. |
| 8 | Featured.com | Same as Qwoted | Publisher's DR | Free tier with limited answers. Expert answers can be syndicated to publishers with a link back. |

## Notes

- Items 6 to 8 are the most valuable. A quote on an industry or trade site can carry a high-DR dofollow link, but each one is earned per request and not guaranteed.
- Duplicate content: on items 2 to 4, publish a condensed rewrite or a canonical link to your original. Pasting the full blog can hurt the original.
- IndiaMART, Made-in-China, Kompass and ThomasNet have free listings and strong DR, but most of those links are nofollow. They are still worth having for buyer discovery in the USA and China.
- Avoid bulk article-submission and profile-creation lists. They are mostly spam.
- Medium, LinkedIn, Quora and Reddit are nofollow. They help reach and brand, not link equity.
